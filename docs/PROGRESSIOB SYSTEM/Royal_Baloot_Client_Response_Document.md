### Our Agreement & Strategy:
You are 100% correct. We have completely stripped RP bonuses for Kabout, Baloot, Qaid, Sawa, and Daily Missions from the ranking formula. They are now redirected to XP and Golden Cards.

### Why Your Philosophy Protects Competitive Integrity:
1. **Baloot (K+Q of Trump):** Pure card deal luck. Awarding RP rewards random dealing rather than tactical mastery.
2. **Kabout (Sweep):** Heavily dependent on trump card dominance. Should be celebrated with coins/XP, not rank skewing.
3. **Qaid & Sawa Freedom:** If players lose or gain RP on Qaid/Sawa, they develop anxiety and hesitate to use them. Decoupling them keeps them as natural, pure gameplay tools.
4. **Daily Missions:** Should reward daily habit, not leaderboard status. Grinders playing 10 hours a day must not outrank high-skill players who play 30 minutes a day.

### Key Clarification:
The Best-of-3 Promotion Series and the Royal Cup are **two completely separate systems** that serve different purposes and run in parallel:

* **Best-of-3 Promotion Series:** An automatic, **100% free** milestone gate inside Normal Ranked Play. When a player fills Sub-Level III of any rank, their RP freezes and they must win a Best-of-3 series to graduate to the next main rank. It costs zero tickets, zero Golden Cards, and cannot be bought.
* **Royal Cup:** An optional, **ticket-gated premium arena**. Players enter by choice using a Cup ticket. Matches are high stakes (±35 RP), faster, and feed into a dedicated seasonal competition track.

| **Ticket Mechanics & Subscription** | 1 ticket per entry. Active subscribers receive **2 free tickets daily** at midnight (expire after 24h). Extra tickets cost **100 Golden Cards**. |

* **Tournament Name & Room:** Fully customized by host.
* **Bracket Size:** 4, 8, 16, or 32 teams (Single Elimination, Double Elimination, or Swiss).

```

Winners payout: 7,200 GC distributed to top finishers.
Platform revenue: 800 GC retained by Royal Baloot.
```

## POINT 7: 2v2 RP Calculation & Bot Handling

> **Client Directive:**  
> *"Because Baloot is a 2v2 game, we also need to define exactly how player strength is compared when calculating RP... For example: Team A average 1,900 vs Team B average 2,200. Was your current RP system already designed with team-average strength in mind? The same applies to bots."*

### Our Agreement & Strategy:
Yes. The system has been formalized using the **Team-Average Elo Delta** formula. Both teammates on the same team always receive the exact same RP change.

### The Team-Average Formula:
```
1. Team A Avg = (Player 1 RP + Player 2 RP) / 2
2. Team B Avg = (Player 3 RP + Player 4 RP) / 2
3. Map averages to Tier Index (0 = مبتدئ, 1 = متوسط, 2 = متقدم, 3 = محترف, 4 = خبير, 5 = أمير)
4. Tier Gap = Opponent Tier Index - My Tier Index  [-3 to +3]
5. RP Formula = Base 20 + (Tier Gap × 4)

Example from your brief:
• Team A: (2,000 + 1,800) / 2 = 1,900 RP → محترف (Index 3)
• Team B: (2,300 + 2,100) / 2 = 2,200 RP → محترف (Index 3)
• If Team B was 2,400 RP (Tier 4 - خبير): Gap = +1.
• Team A victory awards: 20 + (1 × 4) = +24 RP to BOTH Player 1 and Player 2.
• Team B defeat costs: -24 RP to BOTH Player 3 and Player 4.
```

### Bot & Anti-Exploit Rules:
* **Bot Indexing:** Bots are strictly benchmarked as Tier 0 (مبتدئ).
* **Win Against Bots:** Yields a flat **+5 RP** (or 0 RP in high ranks), preventing players from farming AI opponents.
* **Loss Against Bots:** Incurs **0 RP loss**.
* **Royal Cup Prohibition:** Bots are **strictly forbidden** in Royal Cup matches. Every Cup match is 100% human vs. human.
